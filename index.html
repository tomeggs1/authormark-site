AuthorMark

A reading checklist for the authors you collect.

AuthorMark lists every book by an author, in the order they were published. Tick off the ones you own and the ones you've read, and see at a glance what's still missing from your shelf.

Free

  • Complete book lists for each author
  • Own and Read checkmarks
  • Filter, sort and switch between list and card views

AuthorMark Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Star ratings, notes and the date you last read each book
  • Track your copies: edition, signed, when you got it and what you paid
  • Collection summaries for each author
  • Print your checklist
  • Send feedback and request new authors

Support

Questions, bug reports or an author you'd like added? Email AuthorMark-feedback@gmail.com. Pro readers can also send feedback from inside the app.

Privacy

Your collection stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.
